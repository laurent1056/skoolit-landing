The real problem

The parent coordination problem is bigger than just school.

A parent with two kids can be managing a school portal, a classroom app, district emails, a school calendar, payment portals, a soccer coach on Gmail, a piano teacher on Yahoo, a travel baseball group chat — and none of it talks to each other.

Skoolit closes the loop. Connect your inbox once via Gmail, iCloud, or Outlook — then tell it who to watch. Skoolit monitors automatically from that point forward. No forwarding setup required.

Even organized parents get burned by:

  • 📋A permission slip buried in a Friday afternoon email
  • ⚽A practice rescheduled in a reply-all from two weeks ago
  • 📚A marking period closing with three missing assignments nobody flagged
  • 🎵A recital time that changed and nobody remembered to tell the nanny
  • 💳A payment deadline that expired while it sat unread
