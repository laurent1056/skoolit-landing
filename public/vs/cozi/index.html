When Cozi is the right choice

Cozi is a well-designed app and it's genuinely useful for families who want a single shared view of the week, grocery lists, and a family journal. If your scheduling pain is mainly about keeping everyone on the same page once events are entered, Cozi does that well.

It's also a good fit if you're looking for something lightweight with a free tier that covers basic calendar sharing without a subscription.

When Skoolit is the right choice

If your actual problem is keeping up with what's coming in — the emails from school, the coach updates, the studio newsletters, the payment deadlines — Cozi doesn't help with that. It's a place to put events, not a tool to find them.

Skoolit is built for the parent who's tired of reading 90 emails a month and still missing the practice time change buried in reply-all thread number 14. It monitors your inbox, understands what's a schedule change and what's a newsletter, and handles the calendar for you. You spend less time managing information and more time being present.

If you have two or more kids, multiple extracurriculars, a co-parent, or a caregiver who needs to stay in the loop, Skoolit's coordination layer is where it especially shines — it's the part Cozi was never designed to do.
