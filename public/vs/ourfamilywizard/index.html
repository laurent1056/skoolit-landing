When OurFamilyWizard is the right choice

OurFamilyWizard is designed specifically for divorced and separated co-parents who need a structured, documented communication channel — one that can be referenced in legal proceedings. If you need timestamped message logs, expense reimbursement tracking, or a custody-aware calendar that a family law attorney or mediator can review, OurFamilyWizard is built for exactly that.

It's not a general-purpose family organizer — it's a legal tool that happens to have calendar and scheduling features. If that's the context you're in, it's the right tool for that job.

When Skoolit is the right choice

If your problem is the volume of school and activity emails — the 90-plus messages per month from schools, coaches, studios, and leagues, and the deadlines and schedule changes buried inside them — OurFamilyWizard doesn't help with that. It has no inbox monitoring, no email parsing, and no automatic calendar population.

Skoolit solves a different problem: getting information out of your inbox and into your calendar automatically, surfacing what needs action, and keeping every parent and caregiver in the loop without everyone needing to use the same app. Each co-parent can connect their own inbox independently.

Many co-parents use both: OurFamilyWizard for legal coordination and documented communication, and Skoolit for day-to-day school and activity inbox management. They address different layers of the co-parenting stack.
