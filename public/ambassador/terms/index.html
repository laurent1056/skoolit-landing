Ambassador Commission Terms

Last updated July 6, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, how commission works in the Skoolit Ambassador Program.

Commission rate

You earn 25% of the subscription revenue from each family you refer to Skoolit.

Commission window

Commission is paid for 3 months per referred family, beginning when that family's paid subscription starts.

How the 3-month window works

The 3-month window applies separately to each family you refer. It renews every time you bring in a new signup — it is not a single 3-month total shared across all of your referrals.

Refer one family in January and another in June, and each earns you commission for its own 3 months. The more families you bring in, the more separate 3-month windows you have running.

Eligibility

Every approved ambassador qualifies for these terms. There is no first-cohort, first-30, or spot-count restriction.

Adjustments

These rates and windows are provisional. They are not yet locked to a final customer-lifetime-value model, and we reserve the right to adjust the commission rate and window going forward. Any change applies from the date it takes effect and will not claw back commission you have already earned.
