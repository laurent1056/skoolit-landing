The gap ClassDojo leaves

What ClassDojo doesn't do for parents

ClassDojo is a classroom communication tool. Teachers use it to share updates, photos, and behavior notes directly with parents through the app. For what it's designed to do, it works well. But it was designed to solve a teacher's problem — not a parent's.

As a parent, your school communication doesn't live only in ClassDojo. It also lives in your Gmail, your iCloud Mail, your Outlook inbox. The permission slip for the field trip arrived in a school newsletter email. The coach's schedule change came from a Gmail address. The PTA fundraiser deadline was in an email from the school office. ClassDojo has no visibility into any of this.

Better together

Why parents use Skoolit alongside ClassDojo

ClassDojo and Skoolit are not competitors. They solve genuinely different problems, and the parents who use both find that the two tools complement each other cleanly.

ClassDojo covers what happens inside the classroom. Your child's teacher posts updates, shares photos from the school day, and communicates with parents through the app. ClassDojo is the teacher's tool, and it's good at what it does.

Skoolit covers everything that lands in your inbox. The permission slip email from the front office. The coach's practice schedule. The PTA fundraiser deadline. The school closure notification. The field trip form with a link. None of this goes through ClassDojo — all of it goes through your email. Skoolit is the layer that reads that email and makes it actionable.

Most families find the split natural once they see it: ClassDojo for what's happening inside your child's classroom, Skoolit for all the administrative coordination that lands in your personal inbox. Together, they cover the full picture of school and activity communication — without requiring you to manually move anything.

Getting started

Set up Skoolit in three steps

Skoolit is designed to be running in under two minutes. No school adoption required, no teacher coordination, no email forwarding rules.

1

Connect your inbox

Authorize Skoolit to read your Gmail, iCloud Mail, or Outlook account via OAuth. Read-only access — Skoolit never sends email on your behalf and never stores your inbox beyond the school and activity items it extracts.

2

Add your children

Enter each child's name, school, and grade. Skoolit backfills the last 30 days of your inbox automatically, matching emails to the right child and building your family calendar from events it finds.

3

Invite your co-parent or caregiver

Link a co-parent's account so both email streams feed the same family calendar. Share a read-only magic link with a grandparent, nanny, or caregiver — no account needed on their end.
